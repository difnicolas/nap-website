In-Game Notices

Messages ready to paste into the game. Most come in two sizes: a short version that fits the 300-character alliance notice, and a longer one for the 500-character group message. The copy button is at the top of each box.

The tag list and the blacklist fill themselves in. They are pulled from the directory and the blacklist every time this page loads, so a notice copied today is current today. The counter above each box is live — edit the text before copying if you go over the limit.

Posting them

The alliance notice is the one people actually read, so keep it current: repost the tag notice after any alliance joins or leaves, the All Out notice at the start of every All Out window, and the blacklist notice whenever a name is added or a listing ends.

If you shorten one, keep the parts that stop arguments later: which tags are covered, that Town Centres are never open, and that being blacklisted is not permission to attack.
