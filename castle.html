Castle Battles

The throne rotates. Rather than letting one alliance sit on the Castle all season, NAP alliances take their turn holding it — and everyone else is welcome to come and make them work for it.

How the rotation works

KvK Castles

The top two NAP alliances rotate ownership of the Castle during KvK. These are the windows where the Castle matters most to the kingdom's result, so they go to the alliances best placed to hold it.

Non-KvK Castles

Every other NAP alliance rotates ownership of the Castle outside KvK. That is the point of the rotation: members who would never come out on top of an open free-for-all still get their turn at the throne.

Holding it

The alliance whose turn it is has one job: hold the Castle for 2.5 hours to secure the victory. Turn up in numbers, hold the timer out, and the win is yours. Fail to hold it and the turn is simply spent — the rotation moves on.

Rules during a NAP castle battle

  1. No rallies on the Castle For the duration of the battle, nobody rallies the Castle. This is what keeps a turn from being ended in a single co-ordinated hit, and it is what makes the rotation worth having.
  2. Solo attacks are allowed On the turrets and on the Castle itself. The battle is meant to be fought — the holding alliance is expected to defend, not to be handed 2.5 hours of quiet.
  3. Everyone may take part Alliances outside the NAP are welcome to join in by taking turrets and fighting it out. You do not need to be in the pact to contest a castle battle.

The rotation is an agreement, not a guarantee. Whose turn it is decides who is expected to take the Castle — it does not stop anyone from contesting it. Turn up and hold it.

If someone breaks the rules

Rallying the Castle during a NAP castle battle is handled like any other violation: screenshot it, send it to your own R4 or R5, and let them take it up. Never retaliate before reporting and getting approval. The process is on the rules page.
