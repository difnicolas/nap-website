Kingdom 1886

Rules & Punishments

The list below is the official set of NAP violations. It applies to every player in an alliance that has signed the pact, including farm and academy alliances. Not knowing the list is not a defence — R4s and R5s are expected to pass it on.

Violations

  1. Attacking a NAP member's Town Centre No solo hits and no rallies against the city of any player in a NAP alliance, at any time, regardless of power, alliance or how new they are. This includes players who are shieldless, offline or newly migrated in.
  2. Attacking a NAP member's RSS tile outside of All Out Marches out gathering resources are protected. Tile hits on NAP members are only permitted during All Out — outside that window, hitting a gatherer is treated the same as hitting their city.
  3. Attacking a non-NAP member's Town Centre more than twice Players not covered by the pact may be hit, but no more than two times. Beyond that it becomes farming, and it is a violation — the limit exists so nobody gets driven off the kingdom.
  4. Attacking NAP alliance structures, banners or buildings Alliance banners, flags and built structures belonging to a NAP alliance are off limits. Do not attack them, and do not plant over them to force a fight.
  5. Verbal, sexual or racial harassment of any player In any channel — world, alliance or direct message — and against any player, including players from other kingdoms. There is no warning tier for this one.
  6. Misuse of the Minister of Justice position The position exists to serve the kingdom, not the holder's alliance or friends. Using it to shield rule-breakers, to punish rivals, or to hand out favours is a violation by the holder and by whoever appointed them to do it.
  7. Stealing a Fort or Sanctuary from the assigned list If an objective is assigned to an alliance on the assignments board, no other signatory takes it. Claims go through the council before the rotation, not by rally.
  8. Poaching members of other alliances by direct message Do not DM another alliance's players to recruit them. Players are free to move on their own, and open recruitment in world chat is fine — going into someone else's roster is not.
  9. Harboring a NAP blacklisted member Anyone on the blacklist may not be given a home in a NAP alliance — main, farm or academy — while their listing is active. Accepting them makes the alliance answerable for them.
  10. Breaking the KvK rules we outline We negotiate a fresh agreement with each new server every KvK window, so the terms change every matchup — what was allowed last KvK may be a violation this one. Check the current KvK ruling before the window opens and again before you march. Council instructions on targets, timings, shield calls and rally coordination are binding for the duration of the event.
  11. Abusing the system to get around the rules Leaving your alliance to make a hit and rejoining afterwards, or using a farm account to do what your main cannot, is treated as the original violation plus a deliberate attempt to evade it. Both accounts are held responsible, and so is the R5 who allowed it.

When in doubt, don't. If you cannot tell whether a hit is allowed, ask in kingdom chat before you march. Nobody has ever been punished for asking.

Current KvK ruling

Kingdom 1886 negotiates a separate agreement with each new server at the start of every KvK window. Those terms are posted below and override nothing on this page — the NAP still applies inside our own kingdom throughout. Always look for the current ruling before a KvK window; do not assume last matchup's terms carry over.

What is still allowed

The NAP is an internal truce, not a peace treaty with the world. All of the following are fair game:

Kingdom vs Kingdom

Players from other kingdoms — but only within the terms agreed for that matchup. See the current KvK ruling.

All Out

RSS tile hits on NAP members are permitted during All Out, and only then. Town Centres stay protected throughout.

Non-NAP players

Up to two Town Centre attacks each. Their tiles and marches are open, but the TC limit is firm.

Bandits and beasts

All PvE targets on the map. Rally them freely — just don't cut across someone else's rally.

Arena

Hitting a K1886 player in Arena is never a violation. You are asked to use your refreshes to find targets from other kingdoms first — but once your refreshes are gone, hit a K1886 player rather than spend gems on more.

Event combat

Anything else the game itself puts kingdom members against each other in. Nothing that happens inside carries over onto the map.

Also expected of you

These are not on the violation list, but they are how the pact stays workable — ignore them often enough and you will end up committing a violation anyway.

  • Never retaliate before reporting and getting approval. If you are hit, screenshot it and send it up. Hitting back on your own is a violation of rule 1 in its own right — wait for the R5s to rule, and act only if they approve it.
  • Tiles are first come, first served. If a tile is already being gathered, take another one.
  • R5s answer for their alliance, including their farm and academy alliances.

Reporting a violation

  1. For an attack, share the battle report with your R4 or R5. It must show the attacker's name, their alliance tag and the timestamp.
  2. For chat or messages, screenshot the whole thing — the messages and the chat log around them, not one cropped line. Context is what decides these cases.
  3. For anything involving chat, include the account ID. Open the player's profile, screenshot it, and send the profile and account ID with the report. Names get changed the moment someone is reported; account IDs do not.
  4. Send it all to your own R4 or R5. Do not post it in world chat.
  5. Your R5 contacts the other alliance's R5. Most cases end here, with troops healed and resources returned.
  6. If unresolved, it goes to the council — the R5s of the signatory alliances, together with the Minister of Justice, who rule on it and set the punishment.

Report it while it is fresh. There is no deadline, but evidence goes stale fast — battle reports scroll away, chat gets buried, and people stop agreeing on what happened. A punishment handed down long after the event feels unfair to whoever receives it and does far less to stop the behaviour, so the sooner it is raised, the better it works for everyone.

Punishments

Punishments escalate. Each confirmed violation is a strike, and strikes stay on record for 30 days. Deliberate, repeated or large-scale violations skip the warning tier entirely and go straight to the bottom of this table.

Violation Level Consequence
First TC or tile attack on a NAP member — genuine accident or misread Warning Public apology in kingdom chat and full repayment of losses (troops healed, resources returned) within 24 hours.
Repeat attack on a NAP member, or failing to repay a warning Strike 1 Repayment at double value, plus a 7-day ban from joint rallies and kingdom event coordination.
Exceeding the 2-hit limit on a non-NAP player Strike 1 Repayment to the player hit, and no further attacks on them for the rest of the season.
Attacking NAP structures, banners or buildings Strike 1 Repayment of rebuild costs, and the player is barred from the affected alliance's territory.
Stealing an assigned Fort or Sanctuary Strike 1 The objective is handed back at the next window, uncontested. Repeat offences cost the alliance its next assignment.
Poaching by direct message Strike 1 The recruiting R5 is warned publicly, and any player moved by poaching is returned. Repeat offences cost the alliance its next Fort or Sanctuary assignment.
Breaking KvK rules Strike 1–2 Exclusion from the remainder of the KvK event and from kingdom rally coordination. Strike 2 if it cost the kingdom the event.
Third confirmed violation of any kind Strike 2 Player is named on the kingdom blacklist for 14 days. Their R5 removes them from the alliance or the alliance shares the penalty.
Retaliating without reporting and approval first Strike 1 Treated as a violation in its own right, on top of whatever the original attacker receives. Retaliation the council has approved is not a violation.
Zeroing a NAP member, or repeatedly farming one player Severe Immediate open-ended blacklist, no warning. Full repayment demanded, and the player is removed from their alliance by their R5.
Verbal, sexual or racial harassment Severe Immediate blacklist and removal from all kingdom events, with no warning tier. Report it in game as well — it breaks the game's own rules.
Misuse of the Minister of Justice position Severe Removal from the post, a formal request to the King for replacement, and blacklisting of the holder. The kingdom is told what happened and why.
Abusing the system — leaving the alliance to hit, or using a farm to break rules Severe Both accounts blacklisted, main and farm, and the underlying violation is punished at double. The R5 who allowed it answers to the council.
Harboring a blacklisted member Alliance The alliance is given 24 hours to remove them. After that the alliance is removed from the NAP directory and from kingdom event coordination.
An alliance refusing to enforce the pact Alliance Removed from the NAP directory and from kingdom event coordination. The kingdom is informed.

What the blacklist means

Blacklisting means one thing: the player may not be accepted into any NAP alliance while the listing is active — not the main alliance, not a farm, not an academy. Taking one in is a violation by the alliance that does it (rule 9).

It changes nothing about how they may be attacked. A blacklisted player is treated as a non-NAP member and keeps the same protections as any other non-NAP member, including the limit of two Town Centre attacks in rule 3. Strike 2 listings run 14 days; severe listings are open-ended until the council votes to lift them. Current listings are on the blacklist tracker.

Appeals

Any player may appeal through their R5 within 48 hours of a ruling. The council reviews the evidence again and votes; a simple majority of signatory R5s overturns or reduces the punishment. Rulings are not reopened after that.
