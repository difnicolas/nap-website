What the NAP is

The NAP is a standing agreement between the alliances of Kingdom 1886: we do not attack each other inside our own kingdom. No revenge rallies over a tile, no farming your neighbour's city while they sleep, no zeroing a player because of a chat argument.

A kingdom that burns its own troops shows up to Kingdom vs Kingdom with nothing left. A kingdom that keeps its troops shows up together. That is the whole idea, and it only works because people choose to honour it.

The pact in two lines

Do not attack the kingdom

No solo hits, no rallies, no scouting for a hit against any city in K1886 — outside the events listed in the rules.

Sort it out in chat

Problems go to your R4/R5 first, then to the NAP council. Never retaliate before reporting and getting approval — hitting back on your own makes you the one who broke the pact.

New to Kingdom 1886?

1. Find an alliance

Open the alliance directory to see every alliance in the pact, its R5, its farm and academy alliances, and its bear trap times. Message an R5 in game — most of them are recruiting.

2. Learn the rules

The rules page covers what is banned, what is still fair game (KvK, Arena, bandits, agreed duels) and exactly what happens when someone breaks the pact.

3. Ask before you hit

If you are ever unsure whether an attack is allowed, it isn't. Ask in kingdom chat first. Nobody has ever been punished for asking.

Thinking of transferring here? →

Alliances from other servers are welcome to come and look around before committing anything. The server transfer page explains who to talk to and how a move to K1886 works.
