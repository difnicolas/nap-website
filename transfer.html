Transferring to Kingdom 1886

Looking to move your alliance here? You are welcome to come and have a look first. Nothing is decided over one message — the point is to work out whether we are a good fit for each other before anybody spends a transfer.

Why Kingdom 1886

The map is shared out, not fought over

Sanctuaries and Fortresses are assigned between R5s rather than taken by whoever rallies fastest. Every alliance in the pact is assigned a Sanctuary, and the top four by power each hold a Fortress, so the buffs are spread across the kingdom instead of stacking on one alliance.

Everyone gets a turn at the throne

Outside KvK, the Castle rotates. Battles for it are arranged so alliances take their turn holding it — members who would never come out on top of a free-for-all still get their chance at the throne.

Growth instead of attrition

Troops spent on your neighbours are troops missing from KvK. Supporting our members and avoiding unnecessary losses is what lets the whole kingdom show up strong — and there is still plenty of fighting to be had in events.

How it works

  1. Reach out to an R5 of any NAP alliance One of your members contacts an R5 from any of the alliances listed below and opens the conversation. It does not matter which one you pick — they all have the same route into the pact.
  2. That R5 brings the conversation to NAP The R5 you spoke to takes it to the rest of the NAP leadership, so every alliance in the kingdom knows who is asking and what you are looking for. You are not negotiating with one alliance in private — you are talking to the kingdom.
  3. Create accounts here and socialize You are free to make accounts in K1886 and spend time in the kingdom before committing anything. Get into chat, meet the alliances, watch how the pact actually runs day to day, and see whether it suits how your alliance plays. There is no obligation attached to visiting.
  4. If everyone agrees, we help you move Once your side and the NAP alliances are both happy, you get help joining the pact and help getting your people transferred onto the server — timings, teleport logistics, and where your alliance and its farms will sit on the map.

Everyone has to agree. That includes you. If the fit is not right, saying so costs nothing and no bridges are burned — far better than finding out after your whole alliance has spent transfers.

Who to contact

Any R5 in the pact can start the conversation — it does not matter which one you pick. They are all listed on the alliance directory, along with each alliance's farm and academy alliances and its bear trap times.

What we will ask of you

Agree to the Pact

Your R5 agrees to the Pact on behalf of the alliance, and is responsible for their members honouring it — including your farm and academy alliances.

Know the rules

Read the violation list before you commit. It is short, and it is enforced. Nothing on it should surprise an alliance that already plays clean.

Show up

Fort and Sanctuary assignments and KvK coordination are the point of the pact. Alliances that take the buffs and skip the work do not last here.

What you get

A kingdom that isn't at war with itself

No revenge rallies, no farming your members while they sleep. Your troops go into KvK instead of into your neighbours.

A place in the rotation

Forts and Sanctuaries are assigned between R5s rather than fought over — see the current board. New alliances get worked into it.

Room for your structure

Bring your farm and academy alliances. Most alliances here run one or both, and they are covered by the pact exactly like the main.

Common questions

Do we have to merge into an existing alliance?

No. The NAP is an agreement between alliances, not a merger. You keep your own alliance, your own tag and your own leadership — you just agree not to fight the other NAP members.

Can we visit without committing?

Yes, and we would rather you did. Make accounts, get into kingdom chat, watch how a Fort window plays out. Take as long as you need.

Can we bring our farm and academy alliances?

Yes. List them when you talk to the R5 so they can be added to the directory with you. They are covered by the pact and held to it in the same way.

What if one of our members is on the blacklist?

Raise it early rather than letting it surface later. A listed player may not be accepted into a NAP alliance while their listing is active — see the blacklist — so it needs settling as part of the conversation, not after you have moved.
