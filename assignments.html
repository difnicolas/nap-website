How assignments work

  1. Fortresses go to the top four NAP alliances by power The four highest-power alliances in the pact are each assigned a Fortress to capture and hold. Fall out of the top four and the Fortress goes with the ranking.
  2. Every NAP alliance is assigned a Sanctuary There are ten Sanctuaries and every alliance in the pact gets one, regardless of size. Nobody in the NAP goes without.
  3. The leftover Sanctuaries are free-for-all Whatever is not assigned to a NAP alliance is open to anyone on the server, in the pact or not. Taking one is not a violation and defending one is on you — the NAP does not adjudicate free-for-all objectives.
  4. Picks are made in power order Alliances choose in order of power, highest first. That is deliberate: it rewards alliances for growing, and keeps everyone competing to raise their power rather than settling. A stronger alliance picks first, and a stronger kingdom wins KvK.
  5. Assigned means assigned Once the board is set, no other NAP alliance takes an assigned objective — that is rule 7. Disputes go to the R5s, not to a rally. Anything marked free-for-all is outside that: take it, hold it, and the NAP does not adjudicate it.

Corrections

If this board does not match the map, tell the NAP council in kingdom chat rather than rallying to fix it yourself. Taking an objective off another NAP alliance because "the site was wrong" is still a pact violation and goes to the blacklist like any other.
