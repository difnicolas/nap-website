All Out

All Out is the one window where the pact loosens — but only in one specific way. Read this before the gates open, not after you have taken a strike.

What changes

Open — NAP resource tiles

Hitting a NAP member's gathering march is allowed for the duration of All Out, and only for the duration. The moment it closes, tile hits are a violation again.

Still banned — NAP Town Centres

All Out changes nothing here. Attacking a NAP member's city is a violation during All Out exactly as it is any other day — not once, not for revenge, not for a laugh.

Unchanged — non-NAP players

Still capped at two Town Centre attacks each. All Out does not lift the limit, and going past it during the event is the same violation it always was.

The one-line version. Tiles open, cities closed. If you are unsure whether a target is fair game, it isn't — ask in kingdom chat before you march.

Before the window opens

  • Post the notice. R5s and R4s: the notices page has an All Out message sized for the alliance notice and the group message. Post it at the start of every All Out, not just the first one.
  • Expect your tiles to be hit. If you are gathering when All Out opens, you are a legitimate target. Pull your marches in if you cannot afford the loss.
  • Screenshot anything that looks wrong. A Town Centre hit during All Out is still a violation, and battle reports scroll away fast during a busy window.

When it closes

Tile protection comes straight back. There is no grace period and no "I did not notice it ended" — check the event timer before your next march. Hits landed after the window closes are treated as ordinary violations under rule 2.

If someone crosses the line

Same process as any other violation: screenshot it, send it to your own R4 or R5, and let them take it up. Never retaliate before reporting and getting approval — hitting back on your own is a violation in its own right, and All Out is not an excuse. The full process is on the rules page.
