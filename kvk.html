Kingdom vs Kingdom

KvK is what the pact is for. Everything we do not spend fighting each other, we bring here instead.

The agreement changes every matchup

Kingdom 1886 negotiates a separate agreement with each new server at the start of every KvK window. What was allowed last KvK may be a violation this one, so check the current ruling before the window opens and again before you march. Breaking the terms we agree is a violation under rule 10.

What the pact still covers

A KvK agreement is made with the opposing kingdom. It does not suspend anything inside our own: NAP Town Centres stay off limits, the two-hit cap on non-NAP players stands, and the blacklist means what it always means. A KvK window is not a free-for-all at home.

During the window

  • Council instructions are binding for the duration of the event — targets, timings, shield calls and rally coordination. Freelancing during KvK costs the whole kingdom, not just you.
  • Watch the clock, not the chat. Timings are set in UTC; the clock in the header is there so you do not have to do the maths.
  • Keep your shields honest. If the call is to shield, shield. A single open city can hand the other kingdom a scoring run.

Getting ready

The resources page lists the tools the kingdom uses for planning which upgrades to run during a KvK window so they score when it counts, and for working out what to stockpile beforehand.
