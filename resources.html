Helpful Resources

Tools the kingdom uses for planning upgrades, working out costs and setting up bear trap formations. All of them are free to use in a browser.

Before you use them

These are community sites run by other players, not by the NAP council and not by the game's developers. We link them because people here find them useful, but we do not control them: never enter your game login anywhere, and treat any numbers they give you as a good estimate rather than gospel.

Suggesting a tool

Found something the kingdom should know about? Send it to the NAP council in kingdom chat with a line on what it does, and it can be added here.
